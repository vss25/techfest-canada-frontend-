Admin panel · proposed structure

Nine workspaces instead of one long list

Today the sidebar lists 55 screens in nine groups. The new panel has a slim rail of nine workspaces named for the job someone came to do. Each workspace shows only its own pages, with small headings. Event work is scoped to one event, chosen at the top. Every current screen keeps its address and what it does; only its place in the menu changes.
